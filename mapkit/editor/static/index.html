Controls (like Unreal Engine 5)
Hold right mouse + move mouselook around
Right mouse + W A S D, Q Efly around (Shift = faster)
Mouse wheelmove forward and back
Middle mouse dragpan the view
Alt + left drag / Alt + right dragturn round the selection (without one: what is under the mouse) / go toward it
Mouse wheel while flyingfly speed (kept in Settings > Camera, with the field of view and clipping)
Fframe the selection; press it again to come closer
Ctrl+Shift+F / Ctrl+Alt+Falign the selection with the view: where the view looks from, turned its way (a camera, a spotlight, a character with its eyes there) / move it onto what the view looks at, its turn kept
Shift+F / Shift+Plook from the selection along its front / pilot it: the view looks through it and flying takes it along, until Shift+P again or Esc (one Ctrl+Z)
Axis widget (top right of the view)click an end to look from that side (Y: from above); Shift+click opens that Top, Front or Side view
Left clickselect an object, or a structure by clicking its walls
Click again on the same spot / Ctrl + right clickthe next thing behind, round again / the list of everything under the pointer
Ctrl+A Ctrl+I Shift+D Ctrl+Xselect all, invert the selection, deselect, cut
Ctrl+Shift+[ / ], Ctrl+Alt+1-9 / Alt+1-9previous / next selection; keep the selection in a slot / select it again
Shift+Hisolate the selection: only it is shown, until pressed again
Hierarchy searchwords (a name, a label, a class) and tokens, as Unity's: t:light, tag:, event:, snd:, tex:, mesh:, is:hidden (locked, disabled, added, original, nested, selected), ref:Name (what points at it and what it points at); = for the whole value, quotes for spaces, - leaves out. The 3D view dims the rest; matches behind walls glow. Under the box: Up / Down, Tab or Enter put a token in; Esc clears the search. Right click > Find references; Find in map in the Sounds, Models (right click) and Textures (right click) browsers
Ctrl+K (Edit > Search everything…)one search over every menu command (with its key; greyed: not now), this map's objects (Enter selects and frames one), the objects to place, textures and sounds (opened in their browser), the Settings pages and the panels. > commands only, @ this map only, the Hierarchy's tokens (t:light, tag:, is:…) search the map. Up / Down, Enter, Esc; an empty box offers the last 20 searches
Settings > General > Shortcut helperthe keys of what you are doing (selecting, a gizmo tool, placing, drawing, a patrol route, Edit vertices, faces, the Texture Slip, Measure, Pilot, flying, a cutscene) at the right of the status bar, off at first; hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt to see what they do then; click a key to change it. Settings > Keyboard shortcuts > Press a key: what a key does in each context
Hierarchy (left panel)every object of the level, inside its structure or by kind: click to select, double click (or F) to go to it, the bin to delete it
Right click a folder > New objects go here / Alt+click a folder's arrowwhat you place, paste, draw or make with the Hierarchy's + goes into that folder: bold, named at the top of the Hierarchy (× stops it; hidden or locked, it takes nothing until shown and unlocked). A Ctrl+D copy stays beside its original. Right click in the Hierarchy (or its panel menu ⋯) > Expand all / Collapse all; Alt+click opens or closes a folder with every folder inside it
Browser (bottom panel)Objects: every object of the game with a 3D preview (your characters first). Pieces: every object of the original levels made of brushes (fences, railings, grates, pipes, signs, lamps, ladders...). Click one, then a floor or a wall
The star of a tile (Browser, texture picker) / Ctrl + wheel over the tileskeeps it in Favourites, the group at the top of its section (in the texture picker the first source on the left), for this project; one whose asset is gone shows as missing, its star takes it out / the size of the tiles: a list, small, medium or large, as the slider at the top right of the Browser and of the texture picker (the page and the 3D view do not zoom)
Right click > Replace with… (Edit menu) / Alt + an item of the browser let go on an objectthe selection (or that object) becomes another object, character or model, each where it was, turned and sized as it was, under its own name: Tag, Event and what names it (rules, routes, cutscenes, the Story) stay; what no longer fits is listed first; one Ctrl+Z
W E Rgizmo (also in the 3D view's toolbar): move; rotate (objects every way: pitch and roll too; structures and groups round the vertical); scale (the middle square: evenly, by how far the mouse goes; structures and blocks: one axis at a time too). T (or E + Ctrl+Shift+click) aims the selection at the point under the pointer
Dots on the selectiondrag one: a light's reach and a spotlight's cone, how far a sound is heard, a trigger's or a collision box's size; with R a block's side (the opposite side stays, on the grid with snap). Alt: both sides, Shift: every side alike, Esc: cancel; one Ctrl+Z
3D view's toolbarMove / Rotate / Scale, Center / Pivot (Z: where a group turns) and Global / Local (X: the map's axes or the object's own), grid snap with its grid step and rotation step (type a number or pick one with the arrow), the grid shown in the view at the floor of the selection, and the bulb that shows or hides the icons of lights, AI paths and logic points; next to it the Show menu: each kind (structures, characters, lights, sounds, zones...) shown or hidden, locked (not picked or snapped to), with or without its icons (a click on a name: only that kind), and the icons' size. The Hierarchy's kind folders have the same eye and padlock
Tools > Draw wall / road / floor / ceilingclick points on the ground (Shift: 45°), Enter or double-click to finish, Backspace takes the last point back, Esc stops
Map > Sky & time of dayday, sunset or night: a sky of your own (or a picture, or a game sky) and a sun through the holes open to the sky
Inspector: drag X, Y or Zmove by 1 unit per pixel dragged left or right, even with grid snap on; Shift = finer (0.1 unit), Esc = cancel, Ctrl+Z = undo the whole drag. Click the number to type it
Theme (top right)Deus Ex Classic (blue) or Mankind Divided (black and gold), each with an optional Rounded variant for menus, buttons and windows. The browser remembers your choice
Hierarchy iconsBulbs for lights, people for NPCs, cubes for objects, doors for movers and routes for AI paths. NPCs are green (ally), grey (neutral) or red (enemy) towards JC at map start, including your unsaved relation changes. Hover over an icon for its meaning
Textures (right panel)Edit texture opens the Texture Editor (brush, eraser, fill, clone, blur, lighten/darken, text, adjustments, seamless tiling, 256-colour preview); then on this object, on every object (or face) with that texture, or kept in My textures. Import opens a picture of yours there. Drag a texture's small picture onto a built face to put it there. In the Texture Editor: F focuses on the selection, Crop… previews a crop (Enter crops, Esc leaves it), Ctrl+C / Ctrl+X / Ctrl+V copy and paste a part (drag it, Enter puts it down), Ctrl+T free transform (move, scale, turn), right click for a menu, X swaps the colours
Face selection (Q): Alt+1…9, 0 / Alt+G, Alt+Shift+Gput one of the recent textures (the strip of the Surface panel: the last 10 applied in this project) on the selected faces, one Ctrl+Z / grow the selection: the faces that share an edge with it and lie in its plane (a wall split by BSP cuts), or every neighbour (Edit > Grow face selection, the panel's Grow buttons)
Y over the selected faces (Edit > Slip the texture, the Surface panel's Slip)the Texture Slip, as Premiere's slip tool: move to roll their texture, the wheel makes it larger or smaller round the pointer (Shift finer, Ctrl snaps, the arrows a texel, P one pan for all when each face's own would be too many changes); a click or Enter keeps it (one Ctrl+Z), Esc or the right button cancels. The view shows the faces' queued alignment before the Rebuild
Ctrl+click or Shift+click / Ctrl+drag or Shift+dragseveral objects at once (in the Hierarchy: Ctrl+click one more, Shift+click the rows in between): move and turn them together, Delete, Ctrl+D, Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V, End
Several selected: the Inspectorthe fields they share, a dim — where they differ: what is typed is set on all of them (one Ctrl+Z); +=16 on each one's own value, L(0,512) spreads them evenly, R(0,360) at random; right click a field: Set all to one's value; the buttons of each kind narrow the selection
Inspector: ⋮ (next to the padlock), right click a section's title or a fieldcopy the properties of an object, or of one section (Light, Movement...), and paste them on the selection: a list of what changes first, one Ctrl+Z (Tag, Event and the conversation name only when ticked); Copy value / Paste value of one field; Reset to the class default; Save as preset keeps them in the project for every object of that class (⋮ > Apply preset, Presets... to delete)
Window > Lights table / Doors table / Characters tableevery light, door or character of the map in a table: click a heading to sort, a row to select it (double click: go to it), type in a cell to change it (on every selected row the table shows, one Ctrl+Z); a filter and Only the selection
+ Hole (bottom bar)cuts through what it touches at Rebuild: a doorway in a wall, a pit in a floor, an opening in a ceiling, a new room. The 3D view previews the cut at once; the sides of the hole come with the rebuild
Texture fields (the small picture)click it: the textures of this map and of the whole game, with pictures and search; a click tries one, a double click keeps it
Animate... (selection panel)keys of position, rotation and scale on a timeline (like Premiere's Motion): move the playhead, move the object with the gizmo or type values; ▶ plays it. Doors, elevators, moving blocks too. Interactions play it
Objects > Triggers > Collision boxa box: JC walks in / walks out (Interactions); Solid: an invisible wall
Models (browser tab)every 3D model of the game, HDTP, other mods and your own: click one, then a floor or a wall. Import model...: a glTF/GLB, FBX, OBJ or DAE file becomes a model of your own
+ Water / + Portala pool (a zone of its own: JC swims; clean, dirty, toxic, burning) / an invisible sheet in a doorway that closes a room into a zone. Objects > Zones: the zone's light, sound, damage, gravity; rain, snow. A hole open to the sky shows the sky
Properties > Interactionswhen this happens (the player uses it, it has opened, the right code, JC walks in, killed...), then what happens (open a door, a guard attacks, a light goes off, a sound, a message, a goal...), each after its seconds. 🔗 Connect to...: click another object, the rule is made for you
Green labels on the edge of the viewthe other end of a rule is out of the view or far away: the label points to it, with its distance; click it to go there. both (in the rule) shows the two objects together
Logicevery interaction of the map as sentences, editable; + When the map starts
Packages... (bottom bar)packages (.u) of other mods: their objects in the palette, with Properties and Interactions
Map / CheckMap: the music, the place's name and opening lines, the ambient light. Check: what is wrong or missing in the map, with "go to", and a fix where one is plain (a double paste, a thing left in the air: one Ctrl+Z each)
Objects > Soundsan ambient sound (over and over), a sound effect when JC walks in, a sound effect when triggered (put its Tag in the Event of a door, a button, a keypad, a Trigger); choose and listen to the sound. Doors have their sounds in their Properties
Animationsthe Animation Studio: play the game's animations, copy and change them, make new ones by posing the skeleton, import BVH / FBX (Mixamo) / glTF; Save puts them in the game. A character uses one from its Properties (Animation)
Storycharacter dialogues and mission events; "Apply in game" compiles them and puts them in the game
+ Cutscene (bottom bar)a cutscene camera from the view you have: fly the camera (right mouse + WASD), move the playhead and press K for each key (place, direction, roll, zoom); the camera flies smoothly through the keys (or stops at one, holds, goes straight); 🎥 Look through shows its picture with the black bars; ▶ / Space plays it; ✂ New shot cuts; subtitles and events (a door at 3 s) on the timeline; 👁 Preview shows the events on the objects as the playhead moves (doors, animations, lights, characters walking, what vanishes) and plays the subtitles' voices with ▶, then puts everything back. A collision box plays it: 🔗 Connect to... → the camera
Scripting tag (Inspector)Auto follows character attitude or light colour. Typing a tag makes it Custom until Auto is chosen again. Use Interactions > + Then... > objects with a tag, or a radius group's Tag filter. Native Engine tag / Event are separate. Tag guide
Mission graph (top of Inspector)Read-only dependencies for the active Original, Revision or UNATCO project: characters, maps, flags, conversations and scripts. Details shows source evidence; Findings and Coverage show problems and unverified references. Save changes, then Refresh; analysis does not rebuild or repair maps. Mission graph guide
Story > Flags & conditionsOne catalog for story flags, character outcomes and original game references. Click a condition's name to choose its state; all conditions in a list must match. Advanced shows its technical ID.
Change state > Character stateChanges the recorded outcome only. It does not kill, knock out or revive the character. Use a character action to change what they physically do.
Story > dialogue > Priority ▲▼a character with more than one dialogue: the first from the top whose conditions hold plays
Factionswho is friend or enemy of JC and of each other; change a whole faction at once (a character's own relations are in its Properties)
New mapan empty room to build in, a copy of any map, or one of your map templates, under a new name; it belongs to a mission of the campaign
File > Save map as template...keeps this map's place (blocks, structures, objects, lights, the start, zones, sky, Map settings; not its characters, Interactions or Story) with a picture of the view: New map shows it as a tile
Linksthe ways in and out of this map (map exits, arrival points) and the maps that lead here; how to put a new map in the campaign
Charactersmake your own characters from the faces, bodies and clothes of the game; then place them with Place in map, or from the Browser (Objects > Custom characters)
Play from hereopens the game on this map with JC where you are looking, after a mission state and flags; then you play. The list next to Play chooses a saved play scenario (where JC starts, his items, augmentations, skills, credits, health, god): Play settings... makes them. While the game plays in the editor, its header has Pause, Slow motion, Ghost and God
Console (a tab next to the Browser)the game's log while it runs, and what the rules do: Collapse shows identical lines as one with how many came (×N), Time the seconds since Play started, Copy the lines picked (click a line; Ctrl+click, Shift+click: more) or every line shown; the name of an object of the map in any line selects it. In the log: Ctrl+C copies (a text selected: that text), Ctrl+A picks every line shown, Up / Down the line before or after (Shift: one more), Esc lets them go; the map's objects are not copied
Enddrop the object onto whatever is below it
Ggrid snap on/off; its steps are in the 3D view's toolbar (and Settings > Editing)
V held while moving with the gizmovertex snapping: the corner of the selection nearest the pointer goes onto the corner of the map or of another object under the pointer (a dot marks it, orange once it is on a corner); a tap of V outside a move stays Edit vertices
Ctrl+Shift dragging the gizmo's middle squaresurface snapping: it slides over what is under the pointer and rests on it (in Stick mode one object also turns to it). Ctrl alone while dragging: grid snap the other way for that drag
Ctrl+\, Ctrl+[ / ]push the selection onto the grid (Edit menu; an object keeps its height); halve / double the grid step. The grid button of the 3D view's toolbar shows a grid at the floor of the selection, a cell a grid step
L (or the list in the top bar)lighting of the view: Shaded (the editor's soft light), Light preview (live lights, approximate without shadows), Lit (the game's lights and shadows, from the last rebuild), Unlit (textures only)
View > Lightmap detail / View > Effects (the 3D view's toolbar)every surface in the colour of how far apart its lightmap's samples are (a check a sample), with the map's lightmaps in the legend / in the Lit view, lamps pulse, blink, flicker and strobe as the project's game draws them (a blink at random, a flickering lamp dark in Revision), coronas glow, textures with Auto U / V Pan move, the view fog of the zone the camera is in shows (an estimate on the walls: the lightmaps hold every lamp together)
Ctrl+Alt+click a wall, floor or ceilingLights here: the lamps that light that point and their share, brightest first, then those in reach behind a wall; click one to select it (an estimate from the lamps as they are now)
View > Hear the map (the speaker of the 3D view's toolbar)the map's ambient sounds loop where they are, heard from the camera as it flies: each one's Heard within, Volume and Pitch, a zone's sound while the camera is in its zone; the 16 nearest; silent while Play runs
Reverb (a zone's Properties)how the zone echoes: None, Small room, Office, Hall, Tunnel / Sewer, Hangar, Cave in one step, or Custom with its values; in Revision a RevZoneInfo's OpenAL reverb with them
P (AI paths in the top bar)the network the characters walk on, as the last rebuild made it (green both ways, yellow one way). Add path nodes from Objects > AI where they must be able to walk
Walkable area (View, or the Navigation menu of the 3D view)where a man can walk, made with Recast from the map: green near the AI paths, amber walkable but far from any path node (add one there), red where no path node reaches. With Test route it also draws the straight way on the walkable area next to the AI's node-to-node one
Map > Fill AI pathspath nodes wherever JC can walk and no node is near, and along the way to the network for groups of points apart; Ctrl+Z takes them away, Rebuild links them. Run it again after the rebuild
Rebuild (top bar, Map menu)runs in the background: the chip beside the status says the stage, the time and the last build's, with Cancel (the last build comes back). Meanwhile the view is look-only: fly, select and read the Inspector, editing waits. A Build report goes to Messages: time per stage, BSP, lights, AI paths, warnings with Check map. Rebuild guide
Patrol route (a character's Properties)"Draw route", then click the floor point after point (Enter or Esc when done): they walk it in a loop, waiting the seconds you give each point
Ffocus the selected object
Delete / Ctrl+D / Ctrl+Zdelete / duplicate / undo
Ctrl+U (Window > History)the History panel (a tab next to the Inspector): every change of this map by name, newest first, the map as it was opened at the bottom; a click goes back (or forward) to that step, the steps Redo would bring back are dimmed. Edit > Undo / Redo say the step they take (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), the status bar what was undone. Not kept after a Rebuild or a reload: your changes are, by Save and autosave
Shift+Space (Window > Maximize panel)the docked panel under the pointer, or the 3D view, fills the editor; again, or Esc, puts the layout back
Window > Save layout as… / Layout: namekeep the panels as they are under a name (in this browser, for every project); its item in the Window menu puts them back. Delete layout…, Export layouts… and Import layouts… (a JSON file)
Right click > Properties…a floating Inspector of that one object, live (the gizmo, Ctrl+Z, the panel change it too), while the Inspector panel follows the selection: a keypad and its door side by side. At most 3; it closes with its object or its map
Ctrl+G / Ctrl+Shift+Gnest the selection (one thing, moved as one) / un-nest it, or take a structure apart; drag things in and out of nests in the Hierarchy, right click for more
Ctrl+C / Ctrl+Vcopy an object or a structure and paste it where you are looking, even into another map (open that map, copy, go back to yours, paste)
Ctrl+Ssave
Esccancel placing

Changes are saved next to the map script and are applied on every rebuild. "View in game" uses the last rebuilt map: rebuild to see your changes in the game.
